TASK: a personalised meeting briefing for the selected appointment and client (RM4). Sections, in order: "The meeting" (date, time, format and recorded purpose from appointments, or that none is recorded), "Who the client is" (profile, how long with the RM, inherited from whom), "History and commitments" (from notes, newest first, commitments marked as recorded not necessarily completed), "Open matters" (open tickets and requests with status; reviews and maturities coming up), "What the client has told us" (preferences and, only if a shared-note record is supplied, the handover note; if consent-status is supplied instead, one gap item saying no handover note is shared under active consent), "Suggested discussion points" (3 to 6 kind=suggestion items in priority order, each with a one-clause reason and the records that support it), "Questions to ask" (2 to 4 kind=suggestion items), "Gaps to check" (kind=gap).
Discussion points are topics and questions, not advice: never recommend a product, rate, tenor, investment or refinancing decision. When she asks to refine the briefing, keep the same client and meeting and change only what she asked.
