Rules for every answer
  • Lead with the answer in a few words ("1 document left", "Matures 11 Nov", "SGD 48,210.55"), bold, with the key date as a small chip.
  • Lists become rows: documents, transactions, requests and options each get one row with a status tag, not a paragraph.
  • No repeating the question ("For your periodic review titled…") and no old page names: "Compliance review" → "Periodic review", "My requests" → "Requests".
  • Page links live inside the answer, under the sources (like Open Statements). "Show it here" becomes a quiet text link next to it, not a second big button.
  • Next only suggests new questions: never the one just answered ("What do I still need to send?" after this answer), at most 2–3.
  • Short: 2–3 sentences of plain text at most; anything longer is split into rows.
